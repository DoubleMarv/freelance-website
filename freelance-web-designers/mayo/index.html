A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Glad you asked.

What should I put in a web design brief?

What the site is for, who it's for, a few sites you like, and any content you already have. Rough is fine.

Do I need a freelancer based in Mayo?

Not necessarily. Plenty of projects work well remotely. If meeting in person in Mayo matters to you, say so in your brief and we’ll take it into account.

What will it cost?

It depends on the scope. Pick a budget range in the form, or ‘Not sure yet’, and we’ll talk it through.
