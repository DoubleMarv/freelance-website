A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Glad you asked.

How do I find the right freelancer?

Tell us what you’re making, the skills you need, and your budget. The Freelance Ireland team will review your brief and get in touch to discuss suitable independent talent.

What kinds of projects can I bring?

Websites, brand identities, graphic design, development, illustration, and motion. From a focused one-off to a bigger creative collaboration, tell us what you have in mind.

How do I join as a freelancer?

Send an application with your main craft, portfolio, experience, and availability. We’ll keep your details and contact you when there’s a potential fit. Applying does not guarantee work.

Do I need a finished brief?

Not at all. An idea, a goal, or a challenge is a good starting point. Share what you know and we can take the conversation from there.
